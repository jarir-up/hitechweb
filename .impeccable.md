# Hi-Tech Printers — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
**Primary:** Instagram and eCommerce sellers (50–500 orders/month) who have outgrown home printing — they print labels, thank-you cards, barcode stickers, hang tags, and packaging at volume. They're on mobile, they're impatient, they want a fast answer on price and a WhatsApp tap to close.

**Secondary:** Small corporates booking event print runs — brochures, NCR forms, certificates, bill books. Less frequent, higher-value orders.

Everyone else (pharma, textile, schools) exists in the portfolio as social proof, not primary audience.

### Brand Personality
**Precise. Karachi-made. Dark editorial.**

Tension between craft (offset press, 20+ years) and modernity (glassmorphic UI, thermal printing). Not loud. Not a bazaar. Confident press that knows its work.

### Reference Direction
"Explosive depth" — layered dark compositions, high-contrast crimson on near-black, depth through glassmorphism and motion, not through noise. Think editorial luxury meets industrial press floor.

Anti-reference: busy templates, dense info-dumps, cheesy stock imagery, light-mode "friendly" SaaS aesthetics.

### Color System (strict — do not deviate)
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `navy-deep` | `#0E182A` | 60% — all backgrounds |
| `navy-mid` | `#162035` | card surfaces |
| `navy-light` | `#1E2D47` | elevated surfaces |
| `maroon` | `#7E0001` | fills, borders, card accents, button backgrounds |
| `crimson` | `#C8102E` | text accents, active nav, H1 emphasis words |
| `crimson-live` | `#FF5555` | live/urgent indicators only (pulsing dot, badge) |
| body text | `#E8EAF0` | primary text |
| muted text | `rgba(255,255,255,0.45)` | secondary text |
| dim text | `rgba(255,255,255,0.30)` | tertiary/labels |

### WhatsApp Green Rule
`#25D366` is **never used as a solid button fill** anywhere on the site. WhatsApp elements use: `background: rgba(37,211,102,0.10)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(37,211,102,0.28)`, `color: rgba(37,211,102,0.88)`. Green identifies without dominating.

### Typography
- **Font:** Nacelle (all weights 300–700) — body and display. No other typefaces.
- **Display headings:** 600 weight, tight tracking `-0.02em`
- **UI labels:** 700 weight uppercase, wide tracking `0.15em–0.22em`
- **Body:** 400 weight

### Aesthetic Principles
1. **Depth over noise** — layers (glassmorphism, subtle grid, ambient orbs) create dimension. Never achieve visual interest through clutter.
2. **Crimson earns its place** — use `#C8102E`/`#7E0001` sparingly so it always signals importance. If everything is red, nothing is.
3. **Motion is editorial, not decorative** — animations reinforce hierarchy. Stagger reveals, spring physics, nothing bouncy or playful.
4. **Mobile-first, thumb-friendly** — primary CTA always reachable at bottom of thumb arc. WhatsApp tap is the conversion goal.
5. **Show the craft** — portfolio imagery and product visuals should feel like a press sheet: precise, tangible, real. No generic mockups.

### Mode
**Dark only.** No light mode. Ever.
